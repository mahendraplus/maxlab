MCA Attendance Case: A Student's Statement

The Attendance Crisis

During my MCA program, my attendance dropped significantly due to serious health issues - including a liver infection from contaminated water during my daily commute and severe medication side effects. The college's automated online attendance system barred me from examinations when my attendance fell to just 11%.

The Technical Solution

With my background in cybersecurity, I discovered a vulnerability in the college's attendance portal during routine testing. I used this to ethically increase my attendance to 77% - just enough to qualify for exams. I want to emphasize that I:

  • Never manipulated any financial records
  • Didn't access or modify other students' data
  • Caused no damage to college systems

The College's Response

On April 2, 2025, after attending a lecture, I was summoned by an MCA faculty member who made several alarming statements:

"We'll file a criminal case against you... send you to jail... and your wife will leave you when that happens!"

The conversation included inappropriate personal threats and attempts to intimidate me by bringing my marital status into what should have been an academic matter.

My Ethical Background

This incident stands in stark contrast to how I've actually used my technical skills:

  • Helped numerous classmates recover lost devices
  • Assisted strangers with hacked accounts and data recovery - always free of charge
  • Used my knowledge only to help others, never for personal gain

The Core Issues

  1. No Support System: The college offered no accommodation for verified health issues
  2. Disproportionate Response: Instead of academic consequences, they threatened criminal action
  3. Personal Attacks: Bringing my personal relationships into the matter was completely unprofessional
  4. System Failure: If their system was properly secured, this situation wouldn't have occurred

Appropriate Resolution Pathways

The college had multiple reasonable options available:

  • Academic probation or suspension
  • Requiring additional assignments or community service
  • Formal warning with conditions
  • Mandatory ethics training

Instead, they chose to threaten a student who was struggling with health issues and simply wanted to continue his education.
